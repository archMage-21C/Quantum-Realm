# 📖 Scientific Literatures

This folder contains **lists of specialized monographs, Springer/Elsevier releases, and edited volumes**.  
These are more comprehensive than a paper, but more specialized than a textbook.

---

## Entries
- Martin Ringbauer. *Exploring Quantum Foundations with Single Photons*. Springer.
- John G Cramer. *The Quantum Handshake Entanglement, Nonlocality and Transactions*. Springer.
- Seiki Akama. *Elements of Quantum Computing History, Theories and Engineering Applications*. Springer.
- Colin P. Williams. *Explorations in Quantum Computing*. Springer.

---

## Purpose
- Serves as an **index of reference works** that synthesize research progress.
- Provides a bridge between **introductory books** and **cutting-edge research papers**.

---

## Organization
- Grouped by domain.
- Each entry includes full citation.

---

## Usage Notes
- Think of this as a **state-of-the-art bookshelf**.
- Helps identify broader research directions and connect them with both textbooks and specific papers.
