# 🔌 Obsidian Plugin List

This vault leverages several community plugins to enhance productivity, visualization, and knowledge management.  
Below is the curated list of plugins integrated into **Quantum Realm**:

---

## 📚 Productivity & Note Management
- **better-export-pdf** – Export notes to PDF with customizable formatting.  
- **buttons** – Add clickable buttons in your notes to trigger actions.  
- **cmdr** – Run commands directly from Obsidian.  
- **dataview** – Create dynamic views of your notes using queries.  
- **easy-typing-obsidian** – Improve the typing experience with enhanced keyboard shortcuts.  
- **editing-toolbar** – Add an editing toolbar for easier formatting.  
- **janitor** – Automatically clean up orphaned files and links.  
- **multi-column-markdown** – Create multi-column layouts in Markdown.  
- **nldates-obsidian** – Natural language date parsing for tasks and notes.  
- **recent-files-obsidian** – Quickly access recently opened files.  
- **tag-wrangler** – Easily manage and organize tags in your vault.  
- **templater-obsidian** – Template management and automation for notes.  
- **update-time-on-edit** – Auto-update timestamps when editing a note.  
- **url-into-selection** – Insert URLs directly into your selected text.  

---

## 🎨 Visuals & Layout
- **advanced-canvas** – Create and manage complex visualizations and graphs.  
- **enhanced-symbols-prettifier** – Beautify mathematical and symbolic representations.  
- **homepage** – Create a personal homepage within Obsidian.  
- **obsidian-admonition** – Visually distinct admonitions (warnings, tips, etc.).  
- **obsidian-banners** – Add banners to your notes for emphasis.  
- **obsidian-charts** – Interactive charts from data.  
- **obsidian-excalidraw-plugin** – Integration with Excalidraw for diagrams.  
- **obsidian-icon-folder** – Assign custom icons to folders.  
- **obsidian-style-settings** – Customize the look and feel of your vault.  
- **tabs** – Tab-based navigation for Obsidian.  

---

## 📊 Research & Technical Tools
- **etymology-lookup** – Quick access to etymological details for words.  
- **mathematica-plot** – Embed Mathematica plots in your notes.  
- **mesh-ai** – Use AI to generate and manage structured data.  
- **obsidian-completr** – Enhanced autocompletion for easier note-taking.  
- **obsidian-languagetool-plugin** – Grammar and style checks.  
- **obsidian-latex-suite** – LaTeX support for equations.  
- **obsidian-linter** – Format and lint Markdown notes automatically.  
- **obsidian-meta-bind-plugin** – Bind metadata fields to specific notes.  
- **pdf-plus** – Enhanced PDF handling and reading.  
- **table-editor-obsidian** – A more powerful table editor.  

---

## 🔧 Repo & Sync Utilities
- **github-sync** – Sync notes and content with GitHub.  
- **obsidian42-brat** – Customizable themes and UI tweaks.  
- **obsidian-importer** – Import data from other note-taking systems.  
- **obsidian-plugin-update-tracker** – Track plugin updates.  
- **obsidian-projects** – Manage and track projects and tasks.  
- **obsidian-reading-time** – Display estimated reading time.  

This list will evolve as the vault grows.  
If you’d like to suggest additional plugins, please see our [Contributing Guidelines](./CONTRIBUTING.md).
